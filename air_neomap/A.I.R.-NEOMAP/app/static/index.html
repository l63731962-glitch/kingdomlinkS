Getting started

KingdomLink is your church's roster and attendance tracker. Everyone signs in with an email and password set for them when they were registered — if you don't have one yet, ask whoever manages members at your church to register you (see Members below).

The sidebar on the left is how you get around. What you see there depends on your role — a regular member sees fewer pages than a leader, and a leader sees fewer than an admin. That's intentional: everyone only sees what they need for their own part.

Follow-up queue

This is your home page. If you're a leader or admin, it shows people assigned to you to check on — usually because they missed a service. Open a person, make contact, and record what happened (reached them, no answer, wrong number). That closes the item.

If you can register members, you'll also see a Visitors filter here — people who visited and asked to be followed up with. You can add a new visitor directly from that view.

Events

Events lists everything scheduled, upcoming and past. If you're a leader or admin, you can create a new event and mark attendance for it once it's happened — open the event and use Take attendance to mark each person present or absent.

Birthdays

Shows which members have a birthday coming up in the next 7 days, with their phone number, so you can reach out. Visible to leaders and admins only.

Members

This is the full roster, grouped by role. Use Register member to add someone new — you'll need at least their name and role. If you're registering a child, you'll also pick a guardian from the existing adults.

Click anyone's name to open their details. From there you can:

  • Edit details — fix a typo in their name, phone, email, area, or date of birth.
  • Move to cell — move them into a different cell group, or take them out of one entirely.

Suspended members are hidden from this list on purpose, so the day-to-day roster only shows people who are actually active.

Cell leaders

This page lists every cell group. New cell creates one and picks its first leader in the same step. To change who leads a cell later, use the Change leader picker directly on that cell's card — this only changes who's leading, it does not change that person's role.

Statistics & Admin overview

Statistics shows attendance trends over time, plus how many visitors have come through and how many have become members. Admin overview is a snapshot of the most recent service — who's assigned to follow up on whom, and how that's progressing.

Leader accountability

Leaders are tracked the same way regular members are — if a leader misses services, they're flagged here too. If a leader stays unresolved past your church's configured window, it automatically escalates to senior leadership.

Role management & Audit log

Only visible to the Head Admin — the account your church was first set up with. Role management is where you promote someone to admin, demote an admin, or suspend and restore anyone's access. The Head Admin's own role and status can never be changed by anyone, including themselves — that protection is permanent.

Audit log is a complete record of every one of those actions: who did what, to whom, and when. Nothing here can be edited or deleted.

Good to know

Anything you see on your own record — your own check-ins, your own RSVPs — is always visible to you. What a leader or admin sees about you is more limited by design: things like attendance counts are usually aggregate numbers, not per-person lists, unless you've specifically opted in to sharing them.
